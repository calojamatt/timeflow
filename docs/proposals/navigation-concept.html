What changes

Every top-level screen keeps the same four destinations visible, so leaving Reports no longer depends on finding a hidden app-bar action.

1
Always availableToday, Calendar, Reports, and Backup stay in a fixed bottom bar on every screen.
2
Clear active stateThe selected icon and label use TimeFlow indigo with a soft pill indicator; inactive destinations remain neutral.
3
Content stays separateReports keep their export action in the app bar; the bottom bar is only for top-level navigation.
Prototype interaction: select any bottom destination. The screen content and highlighted navigation item update together. On Calendar, tap the selected-date control to open the device date picker and inspect existing shifts for another day. Backup is labeled “Backup” to fit the four-item bar; its screen title remains “Backup & Restore”.
